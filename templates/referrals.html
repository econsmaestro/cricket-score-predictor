The rules of the game

How rewards work

  • A referral must be a genuinely new registered account that joined using your link.
  • They must complete their first successful non-demo prediction to qualify.
  • A genuinely new friend receives 30 days of Pro when they sign up through your valid link and pass our eligibility checks. Existing users creating additional accounts do not qualify.
  • Each eligible referral earns one calendar month of Pro. Rewards extend an existing Pro expiry, or start from now.
  • The new account must have a verified email. Referral rewards extend access; they do not cancel or change recurring Stripe billing.
  • At most five rewards are applied per calendar month, using Singapore time (UTC+8). The final month is clipped at month-end.
  • There is no lifetime limit. Qualified referrals over the monthly cap stay pending and are processed automatically in a later month.
  • No self-referrals or additional accounts. A shared IP address alone never disqualifies a referral.
