Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Cricket Match Intelligence is an entertainment-focused cricket prediction tool with a free tier and an optional paid plan (Matchday Pro). We take your privacy seriously and collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the service.

What we collect

  • Account information (optional)
    If you log in with Replit or Google, we store your name, email address and profile image to personalise your experience. We never receive your Replit or Google password. You can use the free tier without logging in.
  • Feedback and bug reports
    Any ratings, comments, or bug reports you submit voluntarily are stored to help improve the tool.
  • Anonymous page views
    We log anonymous visit counts per page to understand which features are most used. No personal identifiers are attached.
  • Subscription details
    If you subscribe to Matchday Pro, payment is handled by Stripe. We store only your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and your subscription status and renewal date, never your card number.
  • Referrals
    We give each logged-in user a personal referral link. If you arrive through one, a cookie in your browser remembers it for up to 30 days. To prevent duplicate accounts we keep a one-way hash of your normalised email address. We record which referral led to which sign-up so rewards can be applied.
  • Emails
    If you use Pro match alerts, or have Pro access that is about to end, we email you at your account address. We do not send marketing emails or share your address with advertisers.
  • Support chat messages
    If you use the Third Umpire AI support chat, messages are processed by OpenAI and stored temporarily for the duration of your session only.

What we do not collect

  • We never see or store your card details or passwords
  • No location tracking
  • No advertising or third-party tracking cookies
  • No sale of data to third parties

Third-party services

The app uses the following external services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Replit — hosting and sign-in
  • Google — optional sign-in, and sending our emails through Gmail
  • Stripe — subscription payments
  • OpenAI — powers the Third Umpire AI support chat
  • Cricbuzz / ESPN Cricinfo — live match data (read-only scraping, no user data shared)

Data retention

Feedback and bug reports are retained indefinitely to improve the tool. Anonymous page view counts are aggregated and retained for analytics. If you would like your data removed, use the Feedback page to send a request and we will delete your account data, except what we must keep for billing records. Subscription records are kept as long as the law requires.

Contact

This tool is independently developed by Anjaneya Sharma. For any privacy-related queries, please use the Feedback page.
