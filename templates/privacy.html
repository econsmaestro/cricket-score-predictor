Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Cricket Match Intelligence is a free, entertainment-focused cricket prediction tool. We take your privacy seriously and collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the service.

What we collect

  • Account information (optional)
    If you log in via Replit OAuth, we store your display name and profile image to personalise your experience. You can use the tool without logging in.
  • Feedback and bug reports
    Any ratings, comments, or bug reports you submit voluntarily are stored to help improve the tool.
  • Anonymous page views
    We log anonymous visit counts per page to understand which features are most used. No personal identifiers are attached.
  • Support chat messages
    If you use the Third Umpire AI support chat, messages are processed by OpenAI and stored temporarily for the duration of your session only.

What we do not collect

  • No passwords or payment information
  • No location tracking
  • No advertising or third-party tracking cookies
  • No sale of data to third parties

Third-party services

The app uses the following external services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Replit — hosting and OAuth authentication
  • OpenAI — powers the Third Umpire AI support chat
  • Cricbuzz / ESPN Cricinfo — live match data (read-only scraping, no user data shared)

Data retention

Feedback and bug reports are retained indefinitely to improve the tool. Anonymous page view counts are aggregated and retained for analytics. If you would like your data removed, use the Feedback page to send a request.

Contact

This tool is independently developed by Anjaneya Sharma. For any privacy-related queries, please use the Feedback page.
