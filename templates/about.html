About Cricket Match Intelligence

AI-powered cricket score prediction

What is this?

Cricket Match Intelligence is an AI-powered prediction tool that forecasts cricket match outcomes in real time. It supports all four major formats — Men's T20, Women's T20, Men's ODI, and Women's ODI — covering international venues across the globe.

Simply enter the current match situation and the engine will predict the final score, expected wickets to fall, next-over performance, and win probability for chasing teams.

How does it work?

  • 705+ player database — batting and bowling statistics for men's, women's, and U19 cricketers
  • Venue-specific analysis — pitch type, ground dimensions, and historical scoring patterns
  • Phase-based logic — separate models for powerplay, middle overs, and death overs
  • Live match auto-fill — fetches real-time data so you don't have to enter everything manually
  • Momentum modelling — tracks short, medium, and long-term run-rate windows for smarter projections
